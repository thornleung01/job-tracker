LinkedIn and Indeed alerts

This app can't log in to these sites for you (they don't allow it). Instead, let them email you, and this app reads those emails.

  1. On LinkedIn or Indeed, search for the jobs you want, then switch on Job alert (daily is plenty).
  2. Use the same email address you connected in Settings.
  3. Each sync reads the alert emails and adds their jobs here. They never show up as applications.

Found so far: LinkedIn {{ alert_counts.get('linkedin', 0) }} · Indeed {{ alert_counts.get('indeed', 0) }}. Alert emails only list a title, company, and place. Those jobs are scored on that and marked "partial info".
